Journey

Creative Milestones

A cinematic timeline of growth

2019 — Foundation

Fashion Design Studies Begin

Embarked on a transformative journey into the world of fashion — studying garment construction, textile science, and the historical language of dress. The first sketch, the first seam, the first collection.

2020 — Illustration

Finding the Fashion Figure

Developed a signature illustration style — elongated, editorial, expressive. Began creating fashion plates that fused contemporary silhouettes with fine art sensibility. First published illustrations in university journals.

2021 — Styling

Editorial Styling Debut

Assisted lead stylists on editorial photoshoots, developing a discerning eye for proportion, narrative, and the emotional language of clothing. Styled first independent editorial — a moody exploration of feminine power.

2022 — Collection

Debut Apparel Collection

Presented a 12-piece debut collection inspired by the architecture of South Indian temples — draping traditions reimagined through a contemporary, minimalist lens. Received recognition at university fashion showcase.

2023 — Industry

Fashion Consultancy & Freelance

Launched independent creative practice — working with emerging designers, brands, and content creators as a fashion stylist, creative director, and visual consultant. Building a portfolio of editorial and commercial work.

Now — Vision

Creating the Iconic

Continues to evolve at the intersection of fashion illustration, styling, and conceptual design — crafting visual stories that resonate, inspire, and endure. The journey is just beginning.
