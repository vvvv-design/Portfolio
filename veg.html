About

The Art of
Visual Storytelling

A passionate fashion stylist and illustrator blending modern aesthetics with artistic storytelling through apparel, styling, and visual design. With an eye for the extraordinary and a heart for the timeless, every creation is a dialogue between fabric and imagination.

From editorial concepts to runway-ready styling, the work transcends mere fashion — it becomes wearable art, a narrative woven in thread and color, shaped by an unwavering commitment to beauty and innovation.

"Fashion is the armor to survive the reality of everyday life."

Journey

The Creative
Evolution

2024

Creative Director — Independent

Leading visual storytelling for luxury fashion campaigns, blending editorial aesthetics with commercial appeal. Collaborating with emerging designers and established houses alike.

2023

Senior Fashion Stylist

Curated looks for editorial spreads, runway shows, and brand campaigns. Developed signature styling approach combining classical elegance with contemporary edge.

2022

Fashion Illustrator & Designer

Created bespoke fashion illustrations for luxury clients and publications. Expanded into apparel design, developing capsule collections that merge art with wearability.

2021

University of Fashion Design

Completed degree in Fashion Design with honors. Thesis focused on sustainable luxury and the intersection of traditional craftsmanship with modern technology.

2020

First Internship — Vogue Studio

Immersed in the world of high-fashion editorial. Assisted in styling shoots, learned the language of visual narrative, and discovered the power of a single image.
